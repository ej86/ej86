### 01 · External Clinical Information Access Analysis

**EHR · Physician Workflow · Product Analytics**

Analyzed physician-reported difficulty accessing external clinical information to identify potential information-access friction within EHR workflows.

**Dataset**
- 1,725 physicians
- 69 variables

**Tools**

- Pandas

### 02 · FDA AI/ML Medical Device Activity

**Healthcare AI · Regulatory Analytics**

Analyzed FDA AI/ML-enabled medical device activity to identify emerging signals beyond Radiology and examine how AI/ML device activity has evolved across specialties and product codes.

**Dataset**

- 7,088 FDA classification records

**Tools**

**Key Finding**

### 03 · Healthcare Patient Experience Gap Analysis

**Patient Experience · Healthcare KPI · SQL**

Analyzed HCAHPS survey data to identify performance gaps across hospital patient experience dimensions and translate those gaps into actionable healthcare product questions.

**Dataset**
- 246,015 survey records
- 3,183 hospitals

**Tools**
- SQL
- DuckDB
- Python

**Key Finding**

A **14.3-point gap** separated Nurse Communication from Communication About Medicines.
